Motivation: 助听器应用需要对目标说话人进行实时选择性放大，而传统离线处理延迟过高（低至10ms延迟即可被感知）。现有系统缺乏将流式说话人分离与说话人验证结合的低延迟实时解决方案。

Method: 采用两步流水线：(1) 使用Diart进行低延迟流式说话人分离（基于Pyannote，调优聚类参数以降低DER并保持实时性）；(2) 使用Pyannote验证模型对分离出的片段进行目标说话人验证。在数据准备上，使用This American Life Podcast Transcripts数据集，选取主持人作为一致的目标说话人；在模型选择上，对比Pyannote与LIUM的离线分离性能，以及Pyannote与TitaNet-Large的验证性能；在评估上，将预测与实际语音区域转换为100ms二元掩码进行系统级评估。

Result: 在17集评估数据上，系统实现了中位准确率>0.90，特异性达到0.95-0.98（在余弦距离阈值0.7-0.75范围内）。系统在保持实时运行的同时有效降低DER，证明了端到端方案作为低延迟选择性放大下游应用的实用可行性。

Conclusion: 该工作验证了利用完全开源、预训练模型构建低延迟目标说话人识别流水线的可行性，为助听器选择性放大场景提供了概念验证。未来的方向包括进一步降低延迟、在更真实的会话场景（更多重叠语音）下测试以及实际助听设备上的部署评估。

Abstract: We present a real-time pipeline of open source, pretrained models for streaming identification of a target speaker, motivated by hearing-aid applications where latency as low as 10 ms can be perceptible. We formulate a two-step approach in which incoming audio is first segmented by speaker using low-latency streaming diarization, followed by speaker verification against a registered target speaker. To emulate conversational speech while minimizing overlap, we use the This American Life Podcast Transcripts dataset and select the host as a consistent target speaker. We benchmark offline diarization with Pyannote and LIUM using diarization error rate (DER) and select Pyannote based on baseline performance and compatibility with streaming. We then evaluate speaker verification using Pyannote and TitaNet-Large and generate ROC curves to select an operating region. We integrate Diart and tune clustering parameters to reduce DER while maintaining real-time operation. We pair Diart with Pyannote verification and evaluate system-level performance by converting predicted and ground-truth speech regions into 100 ms binary masks. Across 17 evaluation episodes, the system achieves greater than 0.90 median accuracy with high specificity (0.95-0.98) at cosine distance thresholds of 0.7-0.75, demonstrating a practical proof of concept for downstream low-latency selective amplification.

### [1] [Trellis-Based Noise Modulation with Soft-Decision Viterbi Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11184)
*Razieh Torkamani,Hadi Zayyani,Mohammad Salman,Felipe A. P. de Figueiredo,Rausley A. A. de Souza*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出基于网格的噪声调制框架，利用连续噪声功率符号间的时间相关性传递信息，并用软判决Viterbi接收机直接以接收噪声能量构造似然分支度量，扩展到N-ary状态相关方案并用Bhattacharyya距离设计功率电平。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 噪声调制通过噪声的统计特性而非传统确定性信号参数传递信息，但如何利用连续噪声功率符号间的时间依赖关系进行高效序列检测仍待研究，硬判决会带来信息损失。

Method: 先构建二进制滤波式有限状态模型比较硬/软判决序列检测；提出联合软Viterbi接收机，将接收噪声能量直接纳入似然分支度量；扩展到N-ary状态相关网格噪声调制，其中发射噪声功率电平由当前输入符号与网格状态共同决定；采用基于Bhattacharyya距离的方法系统设计功率电平。

Result: 仿真结果表明软判决序列检测优于硬判决处理；并考察了功率电平设计、回溯深度、每比特能量噪声比、符号时长和调制阶数的影响；揭示了高阶噪声调制中频谱效率、检测可靠性与网格复杂度之间的权衡。

Conclusion: 软判决序列检测结合合理功率电平设计可有效提升网格噪声调制性能，但高阶方案需在频谱效率、可靠性与复杂度之间折中。

Abstract: Noise modulation conveys information through the statistical properties of noise rather than conventional deterministic signal parameters. This paper investigates a trellis-based noise modulation framework that exploits temporal dependencies between successive noise-power symbols. A binary filtering-based configuration is first developed as an illustrative finite-state model for comparing hard- and soft-decision sequence detection. A joint soft Viterbi receiver is then proposed, which directly incorporates the received noise energy into a likelihood-based branch metric, avoiding the information loss associated with intermediate hard decisions. The framework is further extended to an N-ary state-dependent trellis-based noise modulation scheme, where the transmitted noise-power level depends jointly on the current input symbol and trellis state. A Bhattacharyya-distance-based method is employed for systematic power-level design. Simulation results demonstrate the advantage of soft-decision sequence detection over hard-decision processing and investigate the effects of power-level design, traceback depth, energy-per-bit-to-noise ratio, symbol duration, and modulation order. The results also reveal the trade-off between spectral efficiency, detection reliability, and trellis complexity in higher-order noise modulation.

### [2] [$hp$-adaptive trees for graph signal approximation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11701)
*Giacomo Elefante,Wolfgang Erb,Michael Multerer*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 提出基于hp-refinement的图信号近似方法，通过域分解与高阶多项式局部逼近，将细化过程建模为二元背包问题以提取更高效的分割子树，并给出后验剪枝策略，配合图到低维欧氏空间的局部嵌入。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 树编码的图分割是图信号分解与近似的基础工具，但现有方法在保持总误差的前提下近似效率有限，需要更高效的细化策略来降低信号近似成本。

Method: 结合域分解与高阶多项式局部逼近实现hp-refinement；将细化过程解释为二元背包问题以确定更优的分割树，从而在相同总误差下提取更高效的子树；进一步提出后验策略，通过优化各子域上的多项式次数来剪枝分割树；为使多项式基系统适用于一般图或高维数据，提出将图局部嵌入低维欧氏空间。

Result: 通过大量数值实验验证算法效率，并细致评估了所应用细化与优化策略的影响。

Conclusion: 该方法在保持相同总误差的同时显著降低图信号近似成本，且适用于一般图与高维数据，具有良好的实用性与效率。

Abstract: Tree-encoded partitionings of graphs are fundamental tools for the decomposition and approximation of graph signals. For the efficient approximation of such graph signals, we develop strategies based on $hp$-refinement by combining domain decomposition with an improved local approximation using polynomials of higher degree. In this way, from a given graph partitioning tree, a more efficient subtree is extracted in which the cost of the signal approximation is considerably reduced by still maintaining the same total error. To this end, we interpret the refinement process as a binary knapsack problem to determine an enhanced partitioning tree. We further study an a-posteriori strategy which prunes the partitioning tree by optimizing the polynomial degrees over the subdomains. To make polynomial basis systems accessible for general graphs or high-dimensional data, we propose local embeddings of graphs into low dimensional Euclidean spaces. We underpin the efficiency of our algorithms with extensive numerical tests which carefully assess the impact of the applied refinements and optimization strategies.

### [3] [Not All Attacks Are Learned Equally in Speech Deepfake Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11763)
*Avantika Singh,Aurosweta Mahapatra,Ismail Rasim Ulgen,Nicholas Andrews,Kong Aik Lee,Berrak Sisman*

Main category: eess.AS

TL;DR: 本文研究语音深度伪造检测(SDD)中不同攻击对模型学习的不均衡影响，提出攻击感知课程学习以改善泛化。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有多攻击数据集训练将所有攻击视为单一伪造类，仅报告总体EER，掩盖了各攻击如何影响学习与泛化。作者希望理解攻击级别的行为。

Method: 先用样本与攻击省略平衡TTS和VC暴露；在推理时测量逐攻击EER，并分析逐攻击训练损失与预测熵以刻画优化过程；据此定义\"高影响攻击\"，提出重放正则化的攻击感知课程，依据测得的攻击影响分步调整暴露。

Result: 攻击贡献不均：部分攻击EER敏感度高、熵集中且损失低，对决策边界影响强。在ASVspoof 2019、2021、ASVspoof 5及Fake-or-Real上，相比标准多攻击训练，整体鲁棒性提升且攻击级不均衡减少。

Conclusion: 逐攻击视角能揭示多攻击训练中的不均衡学习，基于攻击影响的课程学习可有效提升整体鲁棒性并缓解攻击间泛化差异。

Abstract: Speech deepfake detection (SDD) models are trained on multi-attack datasets containing diverse spoofing systems, such as text-to-speech (TTS) and voice conversion (VC). In standard classifier training on multi-attack datasets, all attacks are treated as one spoofed class, and performance is reported using overall Equal Error Rate (EER). This aggregate view obscures how individual attacks shape learning and generalization. To better understand this attack-level behavior, we first balance TTS and VC exposure using sample and attack omission. We then measure attack-wise EER at inference and analyze attack-wise training loss and predictive entropy to characterize optimization. Results show that attacks contribute unequally: some attacks have high EER sensitivity and concentrated entropy with low loss, indicating strong influence on the decision boundary. We define these as high-impact attacks. To reduce uneven generalization across attacks, we propose a replay-regularized, attack-aware curriculum that steps exposure based on measured attack influence. Experiments on ASVspoof 2019, 2021, ASVspoof 5, and Fake-or-Real show improved overall robustness and reduced attack-level imbalance compared with standard multi-attack training.

### [4] [Domain-Incremental Learning for Multi-Channel Replay Speech Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11194)
*Michael Neri*

Main category: eess.AS

TL;DR: 提出首个多通道重放语音检测的持续学习基准，将声学环境变化视为域增量学习问题，并对比多种方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 重放攻击是语音控制系统最易受到的威胁，其声学线索受环境强烈影响。现场部署的检测器需持续适应新声学环境，且理想情况下不能保留过去的录音（成本高且受法律限制）。

Method: 将问题建模为跨声学环境的域增量学习（DIL），基于ReMASC语料的全部24种环境顺序、5个随机种子，评估基于波束成形的最先进检测器，并比较顺序微调、EWC、GPM及提出的任务专用波束成形器（TSB，每个环境保留一个空间前端）。

Result: 顺序微调遗忘严重，旧环境错误率上升18.8个百分点；EWC减少一半遗忘但损失可塑性；GPM与朴素微调无统计差异；TSB显著提升最终与增量准确率。序列中最后一个环境主导最终性能。

Conclusion: 建立了多通道重放语音检测的持续学习基准，表明任务专用空间前端（TSB）能有效缓解遗忘，且环境顺序（尤其末尾环境）对最终性能影响显著。

Abstract: Replay attacks are the most accessible threat to voice-controlled systems, and the acoustic cues that expose them are strongly modulated by the environment in which the attack is mounted. A detector deployed in the field therefore has to absorb new acoustic conditions over time, ideally without revisiting past recordings, since retaining speech indefinitely is both expensive and legally constrained. We frame this as Domain-Incremental Learning (DIL) over acoustic environments and present the first continual learning benchmark for multi-channel replay speech detection, evaluating a state-of-the-art beamformer-based detector over all 24 environment orderings of the ReMASC corpus with five seeds. Sequential fine-tuning forgets severely, raising the error rate on previously learned environments by 18.8 points. Elastic weight consolidation (EWC) halves forgetting but loses plasticity, gradient projection memory (GPM) is statistically indistinguishable from naive fine-tuning, and the proposed task-specific beamformer (TSB) that keeps one spatial front-end per environment significantly improves final and incremental accuracy. We further show that the last environment of the sequence dominates final performance. Code, results, and analysis are available at https://github.com/michaelneri/replay-speech-continual.

</details>
